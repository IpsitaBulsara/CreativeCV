04

What beauty means to me

Beauty, to me, is natural.

Every flower is beautiful in its own way. Every tree, every waterfall, every mountain, every blade of grass — all of them beautiful.

I've realised that the most beautiful things aren't usually trying to be beautiful. They simply are.

A flower doesn't need to be the biggest flower. A tree doesn't need perfect symmetry. A landscape doesn't need to be extraordinary.

That is what beauty means to me: being fully what you are, without apology.

The L'Oréal brand I relate to most

Quiet confidence. Care backed by understanding.

What draws me to La Roche-Posay is its philosophy of paying attention to people's real needs and acting on that understanding with restraint.

I relate to that restraint. I would rather let something speak through its substance than through its volume.

There is also something very human about caring for people on the days they're not at their best. That resonates with me.

Care first. Confidence follows.

08

My growth edges

01Taking on too much

I tend to take ownership seriously, which sometimes means I try to carry more than I should.

What I'm doing about it

I've started involving people earlier and being more deliberate about what actually needs my energy.

02Deep focus to a fault

When something genuinely interests me, I can become deeply absorbed in figuring it out—sometimes at the expense of the bigger picture.

What I'm doing about it

I now pause at natural checkpoints and ask myself: Is this still the most valuable thing I could be doing?

09

My growth edges, continued

03Listening before contributing

I tend to observe before I speak. I like understanding the context and hearing different perspectives before offering mine.

What I'm doing about it

I've started challenging myself to contribute earlier, even if my thought isn't fully baked. The conversation is better for it.

04Saying yes too easily

I'm naturally curious, so when something new comes up, my first instinct is usually to explore it.

What I'm doing about it

I now look at what an opportunity will replace before saying yes to it. I'm learning to be protective of my focus.

I'm not trying to become less curious, more reckless, or emotionally detached. I'm trying to be more strategic about those parts of me.

About me

I'm most myself when I'm paying close attention.

I've always been drawn to the quiet details: the way a place carries its own mood, the way a person lights up when they feel understood, the way an idea changes shape when you stay with it a little longer.

Curiosity is the thread that runs through most of my choices. It makes me want to see more of the world, ask better questions, and keep growing into someone with more perspective and more care.

What matters to me

Creating with intention. Seeing beauty without needing to force it. Becoming better without losing softness. Making people feel seen in the process.
